Visual rules I'll follow

  • No outlines. Shapes are flat fills with slightly fuzzy, wobbly edges, plus one flat shadow under each character.
  • Paper grain over the whole screen, and a second, coarser grain inside large fills, like the sky in your mockup.
  • Faces use dot eyes, a small nose, and optional cheek circles. No mouths except while eating or drinking.
  • Animals: polar bear, red panda, and dog on one shared body.
  • Every scene uses at most 5 colors: sky, ground, navy, yellow, and the character's white or orange.
  • All UI text is Korean, in Gaegu.

How a gift works

The reader only picks from a fixed menu of animation parts and one line, so any input becomes something playable. Today the reader is a free keyword matcher. Swapping in Claude later changes nothing in the app.

  "category": "drink",          // drink | food | sweet | flower | object | place | activity | person | unknown
  "item":     "matcha_latte",   // a known prop id, or "generic" → gift box with name tag
  "label":    "말차라떼",        // text for the name tag and the collection card
  "helper":   "polar_apron",    // polar_apron | owl_courier | none
  "reaction": "cozy",           // happy | cozy | delighted | surprised | sleepy_happy
  "line":     "따뜻하다… 몸이 사르르 녹아.",   // Minimi's line, ≤ 30 characters
  "caption":  "오늘의 선물: 따뜻한 말차라떼"   // ≤ 24 characters
}

Each phase has 4–6 line variations so it doesn't repeat. After a gift, Claude's line replaces the default for the rest of the day.

Tree growth (computed from your logs, never stored)

You doThe tree gets
Write 1 gratitude line1 leaf (잎)
Log gratitude on 7 days (any 7, gaps are fine)1 flower (꽃)
Reach 30, 100, 365 daysThe canopy grows to the next size
Miss daysNothing is lost. Minimi says 보고 싶었어.

Your mockup shows 잎 24 · 꽃 3. With these rules, 24 leaves means 8 days logged, which gives 1 flower. Tell me if you'd rather have flowers bloom faster.

Data

Three tables in Supabase Postgres. Row-level security means each row is readable only by its owner.

TableColumnsRule
profileid, animal, minimi_name, timezone, notify_morning, notify_evening, created_atOne row per user
giftsid, user_id, local_date, text, parsed jsonb, line, created_atUnique on (user_id, local_date): one gift a day
gratitudeid, user_id, local_date, items text[3], created_atUnique on (user_id, local_date). Backfill per question 4.

local_date is computed on the server from the profile's time zone, so changing the phone's clock can't unlock a second gift.

Risks

High

The 3D look not matching the references

This is the biggest risk to the app's feel. I'll handle it with the diorama approach and by spending week 1 on the look before adding features.

Medium

Notifications on iPhone

Web Push only works on iOS 16.4+ and only after the app is added to the home screen. Week 4 includes a short guide for this. Native app-store wrappers would remove the limit later.

Medium

The keyword reader missing things

Anything it doesn’t recognize becomes a gift box with the typed name on its tag, which still feels personal. I’ll log unmatched words so the dictionary grows from real gifts.

Low

Running cost

Zero for now: the keyword reader is free, and Supabase and static hosting have free tiers. Unknown words fall back to the gift box. Adding Claude later costs about $0.00014 per gift with Haiku.

Low

Gaegu at small sizes

Gaegu gets hard to read below about 14px. Small labels will use Gaegu Bold, or a plain font for timestamps.

Still open after this round: the final name (소소 for now) and social features like visiting a friend's tree. Both are parked until after the friends test.
